AgentikTok — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

This policy explains what AgentikTok (the "Service") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We collect the minimum needed to run the Service, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. What we collect

  • TikTok account data. When you sign in with TikTok, we receive the basic profile information you authorize through TikTok's login: your TikTok user identifier (open ID), display name, and avatar. We also receive access tokens that let the Service post to your account. We never see your TikTok password.
  • Your uploaded image. The image you provide to derive your agent, and the agent configuration you build from it.
  • Generated content. Videos and related metadata the Service creates for your account, and records of what was posted and when.
  • Usage and technical data. Basic logs (such as IP address, device/browser type, and timestamps) used for security and to keep the Service working.

2. How we use it

  • To authenticate you via TikTok and maintain your session.
  • To derive your AI agent from your image and generate videos.
  • To post videos to your TikTok account, as you have authorized.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.

We do not use your image or generated content for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

3. Who we share it with

  • TikTok — to authenticate you and post content to your account, via TikTok's official APIs, subject to TikTok's own Privacy Policy.
  • AI generation providers — your image, agent configuration, and prompts are processed by third-party AI models to generate your agent and its videos.
  • Infrastructure providers — hosting, storage, and analytics vendors that process data on our behalf.

We share only what each provider needs to do its job. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of the Service and its users.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, or ask us to, we delete your uploaded image, agent, tokens, and generated content from our systems within 30 days, except where a longer period is required by law. Videos already posted to TikTok remain on TikTok under your control and TikTok's terms — delete them there.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Revoke access — disconnect the Service in its settings, or from TikTok's app settings ("Security & permissions" → "Apps and services"). Revoking ends our ability to post to your account.
  • Delete — request deletion of your account and data at the contact below.
  • Access and correction — ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct it.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, UK, or California), you may have additional statutory rights over your personal data, including portability and the right to complain to a supervisory authority. To exercise any right, contact us at the address below; we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

6. Security

Access tokens and personal data are stored with industry-standard safeguards, and access is limited to what operating the Service requires. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you as required by law.

7. Children

The Service is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors. Images depicting minors are prohibited and removed.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with an updated effective date. Material changes will be flagged in the Service.
